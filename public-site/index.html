DaySumm

DaySumm is a mobile app that helps people automatically summarize their workday. Throughout the day, users capture short voice or text notes about what they are working on. At the end of the day, DaySumm turns these notes into a clear, structured summary, highlighting completed work, planned tasks, decisions made, and any blockers, ready to share as a stand-up update, status report, or client recap.

DaySumm optionally integrates with Google Calendar to add context, such as meeting titles and times, to the daily summary, helping make the generated report more accurate.
