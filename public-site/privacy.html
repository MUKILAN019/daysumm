Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

DaySumm collects the voice recordings, transcriptions, text notes, and work-update content you provide so the app can generate your daily work digest.

This content may be sent to third-party AI providers, including Groq and OpenRouter, for transcription, summarization, and digest generation. Account information is managed through Firebase Authentication.

Data is encrypted in transit. We do not sell your data.

If you connect Google Calendar, DaySumm accesses only your event titles, times, and attendee counts for the current day, solely to improve digest accuracy. DaySumm does not store this calendar data and does not access event descriptions, attachments, or attendee identities.

You may contact us to request privacy-related support or account assistance.
